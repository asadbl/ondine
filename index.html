    The maison

    Made in small numbers, finished by hand.

    Ondine makes two collections a year and nothing in between. Every piece is cut in our Paris atelier from cloth we choose at the mill, sewn by people whose names we know, and finished to your measure if you ask. We would rather you owned less of ours and wore it more.

    Private appointment

    An hour in the salon with a stylist, the collection on rails and nobody else. Tuesdays to Saturdays, by booking.

    Made to measure

    Tailoring and evening are fitted in the atelier upstairs. Two fittings, three weeks, yours forever.

    Care and repair

    Bring anything of ours back at any age. We press, re-line, re-knit and re-sequin at no charge.
